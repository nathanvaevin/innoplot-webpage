Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and your rights.

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Who we are

The InnoPlot Library (www.innoplotlibrary.com) is run by InnoPlot, Gebouw N, Haaksbergerstraat 67, 7554 PA Hengelo, the Netherlands. InnoPlot is responsible for your personal data. Questions or requests: koen@innoplot.nl.

What we collect and why

  • Resource downloads: your email address and which resource you downloaded, so you can get it and find it again in your library. Basis: your consent (the checkbox on the download form). We may use it to tell you about new resources and related InnoPlot work; you can say no at any time.
  • Book orders: your name, email, phone number, shipping address and what you bought, so we can ship your order and meet our accounting obligations. Basis: performing the purchase agreement and legal obligation. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see your card details.
  • Accounts: your email address and name, and your password (stored only in scrambled form) or your Google sign-in, so you can see your downloads and orders in My library. Basis: your request to create an account.

Who handles your data

  • Supabase stores our database and runs sign-in (data centre in London, United Kingdom, which the EU recognises as providing adequate protection).
  • Stripe processes payments for book orders.
  • Google confirms your identity if you choose “Continue with Google”. We only receive your name and email address.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.

We do not sell your data. Only InnoPlot team members who need it can see it.

How long we keep it

Download and account data is kept until you ask us to delete it or you withdraw consent. Order data is kept for as long as the law requires for accounting (currently seven years).

Cookies

We use no tracking or advertising cookies. When you sign in, your browser stores a sign-in token so you stay signed in; that is strictly necessary for the service.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to withdraw consent at any time. Email koen@innoplot.nl and we will respond within one month. You can also complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
